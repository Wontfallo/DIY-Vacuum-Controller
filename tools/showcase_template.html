DIY Vacuum Controller

An ESP32 control box that replaces the one in the AliExpress kits. It measures the actual vacuum with a pressure sensor, runs five patterns from a phone page, and stops safely when something fails. Everything for the build is on this page: the 3D model, printable parts, shopping list, wiring, firmware and a step-by-step checklist.

Patterns

The same pattern logic the firmware runs, plotted over 10 seconds. The dashed line is the target and the solid line is a simulated vacuum.

How the controller decides

  • Every 10 ms it reads the sensor, works out the target for that moment, then runs the pump harder the further below target it is.
  • If the vacuum is above target, it opens the vent valve in 50 ms pulses to bleed it down.
  • Over the hard limit, or more than 3 kPa over your max, it vents immediately and latches a fault.
  • Each vent phase must actually lower the vacuum. Three misses in a row means the valve is stuck, which is also a fault.

Safety layers

No single failure should leave vacuum applied. The hardware layers work even if the firmware is completely dead.

Use rules. Start at the lowest intensity and keep sessions short. Never use it while drowsy or leave it running unattended. Stop if you feel pain or numbness, or see discoloration. If you have a bleeding disorder, take blood thinners or have nerve damage, check with a doctor first. The limits in config.h are conservative engineering defaults, not medical advice.
